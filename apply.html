✨ ⭐ The "No Upper Age Limit" Golden Rule
Reserved-category candidates (BC, BCM, MBC/DC, SC, SC(A), ST) holding qualification higher than SSLC (HSC, ITI, Diploma, Degree) have NO upper age limit for most Junior Assistant, Typist and Steno-Typist posts. This exemption does NOT apply to VAO and Forest posts.
✨ Special Age Relaxations
• PwBD: up to 10 years over category max.
• Ex-Servicemen: max capped at 53–55 years depending on category and post.

📖 Detailed Syllabus

Part A Tamil - இலக்கணம், இலக்கியம், தமிழ் அறிஞர்களும் தமிழ்த் தொண்டும்

  • Grammar: Matching, Meaning, Active-Passive, Direct-Indirect, Positive-Negative, Jumbled words, Error correction, Verchol, Agara Varisai, Homophones, Sentence types
  • Literature: Thirukkural 25 chapters, Naladiyar, Nanmanikadikai, Pazhamozhi Nanuru, Silappathikaram, Manimegalai, Thevaram, Thiruvasagam, Bharathiyar, Bharathidasan, Kalingathu Parani
  • Scholars: Bharathiyar, Bharathidasan, Maraimalai Adigal, Parithimar Kalaignar, Thiru Vi Ka, G.U. Pope, Veeramamunivar, Caldwell, Periyar, Anna

Part B General Studies + Aptitude

  • Science: Mechanics, Matter, Force, Energy, Electricity, Magnetism, Light, Sound, Heat, Nuclear, Elements, Acids Bases Salts, Classification, Evolution, Genetics, Nutrition, Diseases
  • Geography: Solar system, Atmosphere, Monsoon, Rivers, Soil, Minerals, Forest, Agriculture, Population, Disaster, Pollution
  • History: Indus Valley, Guptas, Delhi Sultans, Mughals, Marathas, Vijayanagar, South Indian Cholas Pandyas, Tamil changes, Unity in diversity
  • Polity: Constitution, Preamble, Rights Duties, DPSP, Union/State Executive Legislature, Panchayat Raj, Judiciary, Federalism, Lokpal, RTI
  • Economy: Five-year plans, NITI Aayog, RBI, Fiscal Monetary, GST, Employment, Land reforms, Rural welfare
  • INM & TN: INC, Gandhi, Nehru, Bose, Bharathiyar, VOC, Periyar, Rajaji, Keeladi, Justice Party, Self-Respect, Dravidian movement
  • Development Admin TN: HDI, Welfare schemes, TN Geography, E-governance
  • Current Events: National symbols, Eminent persons, Sports, Books, Govt schemes, Inventions
  • Aptitude: Simplification, %, HCF LCM, Ratio, SI CI, Area Volume, Time Work, Puzzles, Dice, Visual Reasoning, Number Series

📚 Samacheer Books - YOUTH Strategy

General Tamil 100/100 Target

  • 6th-10th New Books: 100% mandatory line-by-line - Nuulveli, Karpavai Katrapin, glossary
  • 11th & 12th: Selected - Bharathiyar, Bharathidasan chapters
  • Old 6th-10th: Classical literature

General Science

  • 6th-8th: Basics Force Motion Light Sound Elements Acids Human Organs - Do You Know boxes
  • 9th-10th Most Critical 70%: Physics Sound Light Electricity Magnetism Nuclear, Chemistry Periodic Carbon Acids Bases Salts, Biology Genetics Evolution Nervous System Environmental Management

Polity & History High-Weightage

  • 6th-8th Social: Diversity, National Symbols, Constitution, Equality, Citizenship, Human Rights UNO, Judiciary
  • 10th Social Vol1 Ch1-3: Constitution, Central Govt, State Govt - Most Important
  • 12th Political Science Ch1-4,7: Constitution, Legislature, Executive, Judiciary, Lokpal
  • History: 6th Indus Valley, 7th Later Cholas Pandyas Delhi Sultanate Mughals Marathas, 11th Guptas Vijayanagar Bahmani, 9th Early Tamil Society Keeladi

💪 How to Become Group IV Servant

  • Secure 40% in Tamil first - else GS not evaluated
  • Target 140+ in Tamil (Samacheer line-by-line)
  • Target 90+ in GS + 20+ in Aptitude
  • Total 230+ safe for OC, 200+ for reserved
  • Daily 4-5 hrs consistent, weekend mocks, no negative marking attempt all 200
  • Follow Tamil Pattalam Tests chapter-wise to full mock
